How chat data is used

Cadence is an AI assistant built as a take-home demo for Cadre AI. It is not the official Cadre website.

What we keep

  • Your conversation, redacted, for 30 days. We save each question and answer so we can review quality and fix mistakes. Email addresses and phone numbers are removed before anything is saved, and everything is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Basic numbers for each message: the topic, which system answered, response time, and cost.
  • Nothing from the "Talk to an AI Strategist" form. This is a demo: the form checks your entries and then discards them. Nothing is sent to Cadre or stored.

Saved conversations can only be read by the demo's owner in the database console, not through this website.

Who processes your messages

To answer, your recent messages are sent to an AI model through OpenRouter, and to Jev (TypeSafe AI) through Vercel AI Gateway to decide what the question is about.

Please don't share

Passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.
