How chat data is used

Cadence is an AI assistant built as a take-home demo for Cadre AI. It is not the official Cadre website.

What we keep

  • Not your conversation. We don't store chat transcripts.
  • Basic usage numbers for each message: the topic, which system answered, response time, and cost. No message text.
  • Only if you ask to talk to a strategist and submit the form: your name, email, and the question you typed, so someone can follow up.

Who processes your messages

To answer, your recent messages are sent to an AI model through OpenRouter, and to Jev (TypeSafe AI) through Vercel AI Gateway to decide what the question is about.

Please don't share

Passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.
